Good to know

Before you write

Do you work with in-house teams or only as an outside partner?

Both. Companies hiring a senior or principal designer can download the resume on the home page; clients get a fixed-scope engagement with me doing the work.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to the product, one decision-maker, and the number you want to move. Design files and research are welcome but not required, I'll audit what exists in the first week.

How do you hand off?

As code and spec files, not just mock-ups: tokens, components, states and a changelog your engineers, and your AI tools, can read without me in the room.
